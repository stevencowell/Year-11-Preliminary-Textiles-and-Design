Section 3.1

Manufacturing methods

Manufacturing is the planned sequence used to convert a design idea into a textile product. A manufacturing sequence organises the stages of production so that work can be completed efficiently and consistently. The sequence may include points where quality is checked before production continues. Where practical action is required, students should follow the teacher-approved pattern, procedure and safety controls.

3 / 8

Section 3.2

Commercial pattern information

A commercial pattern communicates the information needed to interpret and make a textile design accurately. The pattern envelope provides important information about the design and the pattern contents, while the pattern pieces identify the separate parts required. Pattern-piece names and quantities help the user recognise each component and understand how many of each piece are needed.

Section 3.3

Planning and quality checkpoints

Production planning organises the work needed to move through a textile manufacturing process in a logical way. A plan can identify the sequence of stages, prepare required resources, allocate time and provide a way to monitor progress. Recording evidence during production can also help show what has been completed and support later decisions about quality and improvement.
