Properties and end use

Section 11.2

Planning a fair textile test

A fair textile investigation begins by turning a property question into a testable aim. The aim should identify what is being changed and what will be measured. The independent variable is the factor deliberately changed between samples, the dependent variable is the measured response, and controlled variables are conditions kept as consistent as possible so that differences in results can be linked more confidently

Section 11.3

Recording and interpreting results

Textile test results should be recorded in a clear and organised way. Tables should use descriptive headings and include units where measurements require them. Repeated results can show variation between trials, while a summary measure such as the mean may be useful when it fairly represents the set of results. Clear recording makes it easier to compare samples and check the evidence.
