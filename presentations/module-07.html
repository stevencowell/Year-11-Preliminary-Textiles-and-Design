Natural cellulosic fibres

Cotton fibres develop around the seeds of the cotton plant, while linen fibres are obtained from the stem of the flax plant. Natural protein fibres

Wool and silk are natural protein fibres obtained from animal sources. Wool is typically produced as a staple fibre, meaning the fibres are relatively short, while silk is naturally produced as a continuous filament. Wool fibres generally have a scaly surface and natural crimp, while silk filaments are typically smoother and straighter.
