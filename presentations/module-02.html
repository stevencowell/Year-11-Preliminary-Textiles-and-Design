Section 2.2

Factors affecting design

Textile design is influenced by a range of factors. These include trends, culture, resources, technology, environment, economic conditions and consumer needs. Each factor can affect the direction of a design by creating opportunities, expectations or limitations that a designer must consider.

Section 2.3

Inspiration and design development

Textile design ideas can begin with many sources of inspiration. Designers use observation and research to investigate ideas, forms, colours, textures, patterns and other visual qualities that may contribute to a design direction. Inspiration provides a starting point, but the designer needs to interpret and develop what has been observed rather than simply reproduce an existing idea.
