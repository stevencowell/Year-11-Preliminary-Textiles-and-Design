Section 15.1

Knowledge retrieval

Retrieval practice strengthens recall by bringing information to mind before looking back at notes or theory. Instead of beginning with rereading, students first attempt to remember, explain, classify or sketch what they know. Retrieval can be spaced across several study sessions, mixed across different topics and kept low stakes so that mistakes become useful information about what needs further attention.

Section 15.2

Command terms and response planning

Command terms tell you what type of thinking and response is required. Identify means name or recognise relevant information. Describe means give the main features or characteristics. Explain requires reasons or processes and often answers how or why. Compare requires similarities and differences, while analyse requires connections between parts, causes, effects or relationships.
