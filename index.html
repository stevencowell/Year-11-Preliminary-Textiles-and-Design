Visual analysis

Notice, connect, justify

A strong design comment does more than name what is present. It points to visible evidence and explains how that evidence supports a user, purpose or design effect.

  1. Notice: identify a specific line, shape, texture or colour relationship.
  2. Connect: link it to a requirement or principle.
  3. Justify: explain the effect on the user, purpose or overall design.
