Yarn characteristics

Yarns can be described using a range of measurable and observable characteristics. These include count or fineness, twist, ply, evenness, hairiness, strength, extensibility, bulk and lustre. Structure–property relationships

Features such as twist, ply, filament or staple form, texture and evenness can influence properties including strength, firmness, extensibility, bulk, smoothness and lustre. Section 9.3

End-use justification

End-use justification begins with the user and the performance requirements of the intended textile product. The first step is to identify what the yarn needs to contribute, such as strength, extensibility, bulk, smoothness, lustre, evenness or a particular handle. Relevant yarn characteristics should then be selected according to these requirements rather than choosing a yarn first and trying to justify it afterward
