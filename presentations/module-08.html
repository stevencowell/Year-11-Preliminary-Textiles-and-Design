Section 8.1

Staple-spun and filament yarns

Textile yarns can be formed from either staple fibres or continuous filaments. Staple fibres have a limited length, while continuous filaments extend for much greater lengths. Spun yarns are generally produced by aligning staple fibres and twisting them together so that the fibres form a continuous yarn. Twist and spinning systems

Twist binds fibres together in a yarn by increasing contact and friction between them. Twist direction is described as S twist or Z twist according to the direction of the yarn's diagonal fibres. The amount of twist also affects behaviour.
