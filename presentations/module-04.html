Section 4.2

Production communication

Production communication uses clear written and visual information to explain how a textile product is intended to be produced and checked. Written specifications describe important requirements, while order-of-construction plans organise the intended sequence of work. Section 4.3

Digital and presentation methods

Digital presentation methods help designers communicate textile concepts to different audiences. A digital mood board can organise visual inspiration, colours, textures and related ideas, while annotated slides can combine images and concise written explanations to communicate design thinking. The most suitable method depends on the audience, purpose and information that needs to be understood clearly.
