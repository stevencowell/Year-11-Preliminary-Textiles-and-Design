Syllabus and source position

Current syllabus

Textiles and Design Stage 6 (2013) remains the current syllabus for Years 11–12 in 2026. The Preliminary course is 120 indicative hours: Design 40%, Properties and Performance 50%, and ATCFAI 10%.

Open the NESA syllabus page ↗

Course learning package

All 15 modules contain three theory sections, 30 checked questions, three saved responses, a presentation, a video connection and applied practice. The course also includes 20 Busy Work activities and a 10-card folio.

Practical authority

Project briefs, patterns, materials, manufacturing and testing procedures, equipment, supervision and local WHS controls must be approved before use. The theory site does not invent them.

Teacher to confirm before implementation

Five controlled local decisions remain

  • Convert the 60 two-hour planning blocks to the actual school timetable.
  • Approve both project packs and their local WHS controls.
  • Approve each fabric-identification and textile-testing method.
  • Supply the current Textiles assessment schedule and issue-ready notices.
  • Confirm cohort resources, permissions, adjustments and access arrangements.
Privacy boundary: student names, submissions, grades, former-student folios, identifiable photographs, teacher-only answers and unverified source images are excluded.
