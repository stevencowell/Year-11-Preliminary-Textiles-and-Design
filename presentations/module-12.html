Section 12.3

Project evidence and evaluation

Strong project evidence forms a traceable chain from the original brief and research through experimentation, selection, production evidence and final evaluation. Each stage should show how information influenced later decisions. The aim is not to collect everything, but to keep evidence that helps explain what was considered, what changed and why particular choices were made.
